07 / FAQ

Clear answers.
No AI fog.

Is the SELL2AI score an official score from an AI company?+

No. It is SELL2AI's own heuristic readiness score based on public commerce signals we can detect. We do not present it as an official score from OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Stripe or any commerce protocol.

Does the scanner access private data?+

No. The current scanner requests public HTTP/S pages only and blocks private/local network targets.

Will fixing the report guarantee AI recommendations?+

No. Better machine-readable commerce data can remove preventable ambiguity, but no tool can guarantee placement or recommendation by a third-party AI system.

What will paid plans actually automate?+

The roadmap includes continuous monitoring, generated fixes, product feeds, buyer tests, integrations and attribution—not a paywall around the basic diagnostic.
